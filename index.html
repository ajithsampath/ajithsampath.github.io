Research

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Geneva and a member of the Hydrogen Intensity and Real-time Analysis eXperiment (HIRAX). My work focuses on 21 cm intensity mapping as a probe of cosmology — characterising radio telescope beams, understanding instrumental systematics such as primary-beam chromaticity, and connecting these to the accuracy of power-spectrum measurements. Before Geneva I completed my PhD at the University of KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.
